BasaChai PRD

Find a home. Contact the owner directly.

Version 1.4 13 October 2026 Launch: Dhamrai, then Dhaka Platform: Responsive website

1Overview

BasaChai is a mobile-first rental marketplace for Bangladesh where owners post properties and tenants contact them directly. It launches with free rental listings in Dhamrai, expands to Dhaka next, and is built so property sales can be added later without a redesign.

AttributeValue
Product typeProperty marketplace
Initial marketDhamrai (Dhaka District), Bangladesh; Dhaka city next
LanguageBangla first, structured for English
PlatformResponsive website (mobile and desktop)
Brand promiseFind a home. Contact the owner directly.

Problem. Renters today search Facebook groups, personal contacts, classified sites and brokers. Listings are often outdated, total monthly cost is hard to compare, availability is unverified, and reaching the actual owner is difficult.

Approach. BasaChai addresses this with:

  • Easy property posting for owners.
  • Search and filters tailored to Bangladeshi rental needs.
  • Direct contact between tenants and advertisers.
  • Transparent rent, advance payment and service charges.
  • Listing moderation and reporting tools.
  • A data model that supports future sale listings.

New in 1.4. Every requirement now has matching test cases in the individual specs, and seven end-to-end journeys prove the acceptance criteria in section 16. Writing the tests settled four product details: owners can return to unfinished drafts from their dashboard; hiding a listing closes every open report on it; registering a number that already has an account says so and points to sign-in; and searches record whether they started on the home page or the search page. The Master Spec holds the full detail.

2Vision and objectives

BasaChai aims to become a trusted destination in Bangladesh for finding, renting, and eventually buying or selling property.

  1. Make it easy for property owners to advertise.
  2. Help tenants find suitable homes without wading through irrelevant or outdated listings.
  3. Reduce intermediary friction by supporting direct owner-to-tenant contact.
  4. Build trust through accurate information, advertiser identification and listing freshness.
  5. Expand beyond rentals without a complete redesign.

4Scope

The MVP completes the core rental journey end to end; payments, contracts, native apps and sales wait until the marketplace is validated.

In the MVP

  • Public home page and property search
  • Rental results and detailed property pages
  • Location, price and property-feature filters
  • Registration, login and account management
  • Listing creation, editing and status management
  • Owner dashboard reminders and status badges (reconfirmation due, moderation results)
  • Property photo upload
  • Direct phone contact with advertisers (phone reveal, no login required)
  • Listing moderation and reporting
  • Saved properties for registered users
  • Basic administration dashboard
  • SEO, analytics, privacy notice, terms and safety guidance

Not in the MVP

  • Any paid features: listings are free at launch (no featured listings or subscriptions)
  • Agent accounts (owners only at launch)
  • WhatsApp contact
  • SMS of any kind (codes, reminders or alerts), email notifications and browser push notifications
  • Phone-number verification for advertisers
  • Self-service password reset (resets are admin-assisted)
  • Coverage outside Dhamrai
  • Online rent or deposit payments
  • Digital rental agreements
  • Ownership or legal-title verification as a guaranteed service
  • Native Android and iOS apps
  • Mortgage, valuation or property-management accounting
  • Full buying and selling workflows
  • In-app chat

5Core user journeys

Tenants browse and contact advertisers without an account; every owner listing must pass moderation before it goes live.

Tenant journey

  1. Visit BasaChai.
  2. Search by area or location.
  3. Apply rent and property filters.
  4. Browse property results.
  5. Open property details; if not interested, return to results.
  6. Contact the advertiser directly.
  7. Arrange a property visit.
  8. Confirm the property and rental terms with the advertiser.

An account is required only for features such as saving properties.

Owner journey

Listing report journey

  1. A user reports a listing as suspicious, inaccurate, duplicated or unavailable.
  2. The platform records the report and queues it for moderators.
  3. A moderator reviews the listing and supporting information.
  4. The moderator leaves it published, requests changes, hides it temporarily, or removes it. Hiding a listing closes every open report on it, so one decision handles repeat reports.
  5. The decision and reason are recorded for audit.

8Search and discovery

Search is the core of BasaChai: filters combine predictably, results favor relevance and freshness, and unavailable listings never appear.

Required filters

FilterValues
City / districtDhaka District at launch; more added with expansion
AreaPicklist of Dhamrai areas at launch; Dhaka city neighborhoods after expansion
Monthly rentMinimum and maximum, in ৳
BedroomsCount, including studio
BathroomsCount
Property typeConfigurable list
FurnishingAny, furnished, semi-furnished, unfurnished
Occupancy preferenceAny, family, bachelor, students
AvailabilityAvailable now or by date

Search behavior

  • Filters combine with AND logic and behave predictably.
  • Users can clear one filter or reset all filters.
  • Ranking favors relevance and freshness; no paid placement in the MVP.
  • Rented, expired or removed listings never appear in normal results.
  • With no results, suggest widening budget, location or other filters.
  • Support common Bangla and English spellings of neighborhoods where practical.
  • Map search comes later; area names and privacy-preserving approximate locations suffice at launch.

10Trust, safety and moderation

Trust decides whether BasaChai succeeds, so nine safeguards are required at launch; with no phone verification, moderation carries more of the load.

  1. Moderator review of every listing before it is published, and a report-listing feature.
  2. Duplicate-listing and spam detection.
  3. Rate limits on account creation, login, listing submission and phone-number reveals.
  4. Owner-only posting at launch; clear owner and agent labels once agents are allowed.
  5. Freshness indicators and 30-day availability reconfirmation.
  6. Secure image uploads with file validation and scanning where supported.
  7. A safety page on verifying a property and avoiding fraudulent payments.
  8. Audit logs for administrative changes and moderation decisions.
  9. A support channel for complaints and suspected scams.

Verification wording. There is no phone verification at launch, so every contact number is labelled "not verified" and nothing may imply that BasaChai has checked the advertiser. If phone verification is added later, it proves only control of the number, not property ownership. Use "ownership verified" only after an actual ownership-verification process.

11Administration dashboard

Moderators work in a separate protected dashboard with least-privilege permissions and every privileged action audited.

  • View totals for users, active listings, pending listings and reports.
  • Search listings by title, location, advertiser and status.
  • Approve, reject, hide or request edits to listings; hiding closes every open report on that listing.
  • View listing history and moderation decisions.
  • Suspend or restrict users where justified, and issue single-use password reset links (admins only).
  • Investigate repeated complaints and suspicious listings.
  • Manage configured property types, amenities and locations.
  • Review basic analytics and listing freshness.

AAppendix: example search specification

This worked example defines how a search for 2-bedroom rentals in Mirpur, Dhaka, between ৳10,000 and ৳25,000 a month must behave. It uses placeholders only; no real listings are shown.

Mirpur is outside the Dhamrai launch area, so this search applies after the Dhaka expansion. The same rules apply to any Dhamrai search.

Matching rules

FieldRule
Listing purposeRental only
CityMust match Dhaka
AreaMust match Mirpur
Monthly rentInclusive: ৳10,000 to ৳25,000
BedroomsExactly 2
FurnishingAny (no restriction)
Occupancy preferenceAny (no restriction)
Listing statusPublished and currently available
Default sortMost recently confirmed available first
CurrencyBDT (৳)

Implementation rules

  • Apply all filters together with AND logic.
  • Never treat an unknown rent or bedroom count as a match.
  • Exclude paused, rented, expired, unpublished and rejected listings.
  • Keep "Any" filters unrestricted.
  • Show monthly rent separately from advance, service charge and other fees.
  • With no matches, show the empty state; never generate sample properties.
  • If freshness is uncertain, do not imply availability is confirmed.
